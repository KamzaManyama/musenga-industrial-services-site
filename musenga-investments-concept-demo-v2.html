01 / Who we are

A broad range of services, with one clear commitment.

Musenga Investments (Pty) Ltd grew out of Musengavhadzimu Business Enterprise cc, founded in 2002 to provide waste management, cleaning and hygiene services. Since then the business has expanded into building construction and maintenance, census work in informal settlements, and waste-bin supply for suburbs and complexes.

Whatever the scope, the commitment stays the same: superior service, delivered by teams drawn from the communities we work in.

Our mission

Deliver a wide range of services while uplifting local communities: hiring residents first and supporting community programmes.

Our vision

To be an enterprise of choice in waste management, commercial and industrial cleaning, and building construction and maintenance.

Our values

Rooted in good family values: accountability, commitment, trustworthiness, a high work ethic, and respect and empathy for everyone we serve.
